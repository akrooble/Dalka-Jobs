Role Summary

Hormuud Telecom is expanding its nationwide 5G standalone (SA) and non-standalone (NSA) telecommunications backbone across Somalia. We are recruiting an experienced Senior Core Network & 5G Systems Engineer to oversee architecture, EPC/5GC integration, cloud-native NFV deployments, and high-reliability data plane throughput in Mogadishu.

INFRASTRUCTURE SCALE Tier-1 Telco
DEPLOYMENT STACK 3GPP Release 16 / Kubernetes NFV

Key Responsibilities

  • 01
    5GC System Architecture & Lifecycle

    Design, commission, and tune 5G core network elements (AMF, SMF, UPF, NRF, NSSF) across distributed hybrid telco cloud environments.

  • 02
    EPC Interoperability & Roaming

    Ensure seamless interoperability between legacy 4G EPC and 5G SA architectures, including international IPX peering and VoLTE/VoNR codecs.

  • 03
    Traffic Slicing & QoS Assurance

    Deploy custom network slicing profiles for mission-critical enterprise services, mobile money interconnects (EVC Plus), and humanitarian operations.

  • 04
    Incident Command & MTTR Reduction

    Direct Tier-3 emergency response for Core Network operational outages with a strict 99.999% SLA across Southern and Central operational corridors.

Requirements & Credentials

check_box B.Sc. or M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering, Telecommunications, or Computer Science.
check_box Minimum 6+ years hands-on experience in 4G/5G mobile core architecture.
check_box In-depth mastery of 3GPP protocols: HTTP/2 (SBI), PFCP, GTPv2, Diameter, SIP.
check_box Practical container orchestration proficiency (Kubernetes / Red Hat OpenShift / CNFs).
check_box Fluency in Somali and technical English; willingness to be based full-time in Mogadishu.

About Hormuud Telecom

Founded in 2002, Hormuud Telecom is Somalia's leading telecommunications provider, powering communication, mobile financial services (EVC Plus), and digital transformation across the Horn of Africa. Hormuud connects over 5 million citizens with reliable voice, data, and digital services.
