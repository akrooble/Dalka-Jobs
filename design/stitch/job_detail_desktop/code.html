Job Overview

Hormuud Telecom is seeking an experienced, analytical Senior Core Network & 5G Systems Engineer to lead the operational scaling and maintenance of our expanding nationwide mobile telecommunications backbone. Based at the Mogadishu central operations facility, this pivotal role steers the implementation, modernization, and continuous availability of our 4G LTE EPC, 5G Standalone (SA) and Non-Standalone (NSA) core functions, alongside IMS carrier-grade voice architecture.

The selected engineer will act as an authoritative technical escalation focal point for multi-vendor packet core platforms, inter-operator national roaming exchanges, and emerging private industrial networking trials across the Banaadir region.

checklist

Core Responsibilities

  • check_circle 5G SA / NSA Core Architecture: Configure, integrate, and optimize 5G core network functions (AMF, SMF, UPF, NRF, PCF) alongside legacy EPC (MME, SGW, PGW) ensuring low-latency handovers and uninterrupted throughput.
  • check_circle IMS & VoLTE / VoNR Engineering: Oversee end-to-end IP Multimedia Subsystem architecture, session border controllers (SBC), and subscriber data management (HSS/UDM) for carrier-grade voice stability across urban Mogadishu clusters.
  • check_circle RAN Coordination & Optimization: Collaborate closely with Radio Access Network (RAN) performance teams to troubleshoot complex call-drop rates, throughput bottlenecks, and cell-site spectrum reallocation plans.
  • check_circle NFV & Cloud Infrastructure: Deploy and administer carrier cloud virtualization environments (OpenStack, Kubernetes, SR-IOV) supporting containerized network functions (CNFs).
  • check_circle Fault Management & SLA Compliance: Participate in high-availability 24/7 on-call tier-3 rotational incident management, reducing MTTR and driving post-incident root-cause analyses (RCA).
school

Required Qualifications & Skills

Academic Background

Bachelor of Science or Master of Science in Telecommunications, Electrical Engineering, or Computer Engineering.

Professional Experience

Minimum 5+ years hands-on production experience in mobile packet core operations within a Tier-1 or Tier-2 telecom operator.

Technical Certifications

Cisco CCNA/CCNP Service Provider, Huawei HCIP/HCIE 5G Core, or Nokia 5G Packet Core Architecture certification strongly preferred.

Protocols & Tooling

Expert proficiency with Diameter, GTPv2, SIP, HTTP/2 (Service-Based Architecture), Wireshark packet capture trace dissection, and Linux shell scripting.
