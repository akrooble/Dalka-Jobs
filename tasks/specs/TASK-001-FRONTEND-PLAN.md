APIs must reject forged identifiers and unauthorised CV/detail access independently. Privileged roles require password plus TOTP; applicant email OTP cannot grant privileged access. Mock screens must remain visibly fictional and accept no real credentials, CVs or applications.

## Frontend prototype versus a working product
UI design can proceed with clearly labelled synthetic fixtures after its concrete task approval. That does not satisfy live task acceptance. Never place real credentials/CVs/candidate data into a mock portal, simulate successful authentication with browser storage, or claim live access protection from hidden buttons. Use completed backend features as they become available; no need to wait until Task 20 to integrate a ready feature. Task 20 verifies the combined system.

## Decisions needed before affected screens become live
- Identity: provisioning/invites/bootstrap/recovery, role combinations/memberships, session/OTP limits and production email provider. Applicant email OTP and privileged password/TOTP are already decided.
- Recruitment: required CV timing, duplicates/reapply/withdrawal, permitted stage transitions, interview/timezone/rescheduling rules, note-edit/share authority, company rejected-list projection and detail-share revocation.
- Privacy: approved consent/policy/contact content, deletion verification, exact six-month boundaries, active-case and queued-message handling, restore/deletion enforcement.
- Finance: exact prices/override precedence, invoice allocation/reset timing, rounding, partial-payment/overpayment/refund/cancel rules and due-date cutoff. No online payment or automatic tax is planned.
- Public behavior: real closing-soon/exact expiry policy, salary precision, slug redirects and list-reference/deactivation policy. The current fixed-date sample behavior does not decide these.

## Edge Cases
Role revoked while a panel is open; application/job expires while submitting; rejected candidate still has an old detail link; expired session during upload or invoice editing; double submit/payment record; private data in shared caches; unsupported CV; interrupted deletion; deleted records reappearing after restore; mixed-currency totals; referenced managed-list removal. APIs enforce correctness; frontend must show honest recoverable feedback.
